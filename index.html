Reel Privacy Policy

Reel — A Unified Media Player
Effective: 4 October 2026

In short: Reel is designed to be local-first. Reel V1 does not require a Reel account and does not use a Reel-operated cloud service to store your profiles, media-source credentials, viewing history or preferences.

1. What Reel stores on your device

To provide the features you configure, Reel may store information locally on your device, including:

  • the media sources you add, including connection details and credentials or access tokens where a source requires them;
  • profiles, app settings and preferences;
  • viewing history, resume positions, favourites and Continue Watching information;
  • cached catalogue, programme-guide, metadata and artwork information; and
  • local diagnostic information used to operate and troubleshoot the app.

Reel does not operate a cloud account service for V1. This locally stored information is not uploaded to a Reel-operated account or cloud database.

2. Connections to your media sources

Reel connects directly to media sources that you choose and configure. Information needed to authenticate to a source is sent to that source when necessary to access the media you have configured. Reel does not send your source credentials to metadata providers.

The operator of a media source may receive standard network information such as your IP address and request details as part of that connection. How that source handles information is governed by its own privacy practices.

3. Metadata, artwork and programme information

Reel may contact third-party information services to obtain details such as titles, descriptions, age ratings, cast information, artwork and programme-guide data. Depending on the feature and your settings, these services can include services such as TVmaze, Wikidata, Wikimedia Commons, TMDB when enabled by the viewer, and programme-information services.

Requests may contain the title, programme name, media identifier or other search information needed to find the correct metadata. Like most internet services, those providers may also receive standard network information such as your IP address. Their own privacy policies apply to information they process.

4. Diagnostics and crash reporting

At the effective date of this policy, Reel does not operate a remote crash-reporting service. If Reel introduces optional remote diagnostics, it will be opt-in and off by default. Before such reporting is enabled, Reel will identify what is sent and update this policy as necessary.

Reel's design requirement for any optional diagnostic reporting is to exclude media-source URLs, credentials, authentication tokens, channel or catalogue lists, and media titles from submitted reports.

5. Payments, advertising and sale of data

Reel V1 is free and Reel does not collect payment-card information. Reel does not sell your personal information or source credentials and does not use your locally stored viewing information for targeted advertising.

6. Data retention and deletion

Information stored by Reel on your device remains there until it is removed through Reel, the device's app-data controls, or by uninstalling the app, subject to the behaviour of the device platform. Information processed by a media source or metadata provider is subject to that third party's own retention practices.

7. Security

Reel is designed so that credentials and source configuration stay on the viewer's device except when they must be transmitted to the source the viewer configured. No internet-connected application or device can guarantee absolute security, so viewers should protect their device and source accounts and use trusted networks and services.

8. Changes to this policy

If Reel's data handling materially changes — for example, if a Reel-operated cloud service or optional remote diagnostics is introduced — this policy will be updated before or alongside that change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised, and the public Git history for this policy provides a record of changes.

9. Privacy questions

For privacy questions about Reel, use the developer support contact shown on Reel's app-store listing. Please do not send credentials, access tokens, private source URLs or other sensitive information in a public support message.

10. This website

This privacy-policy page is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard web request information when you visit this page. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for details.
